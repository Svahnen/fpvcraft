## Where the contents come from

Everything in this repository was written or generated by the project's authors:

* **Code**: the flight library (`src/`, `include/`) and the Minecraft mod (`adapters/minecraft-fabric/`) are original
  implementations. The physics is standard rigid-body quadcopter mechanics (thrust from motor and propeller,
  battery voltage sag, aerodynamic drag, gravity, ground effect). The stick-rate profiles (Betaflight, Actual, KISS,
  Raceflight, Quickrate) are implemented from their publicly documented formulas; no flight-controller source code was copied.
* **Drone presets** (`data/presets/drone_*.json`): independent estimates for common real-world hardware classes
  (for example a 5-inch quad with 2207-size motors on 6S), tuned for plausible hover throttle, top speed and acceleration.
* **Curves and aerodynamic tables** (`data/presets/curves.json`, `drag_*.json`): generated by the scripts in `tools/bake/`
  from closed-form formulas and from a simple box model of the mod's own drone.
* **Sounds**: synthesized from scratch by `tools/audio/synth_drone.py` (additive synthesis and shaped noise).
  No recorded, sampled or third-party audio is used.
* **Drone model in Minecraft**: drawn from hand-coded coloured boxes; the mod ships no textures or 3D model files.
* **Screenshots and demo video**: recorded by the authors from this mod running in Minecraft.

The flight feel was developed by studying how real FPV drones and FPV simulators behave. The repository contains no code,
assets, data files or text taken from any other game or simulator.
